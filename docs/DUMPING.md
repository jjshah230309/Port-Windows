# Dumping your own media

Attic never downloads games or firmware. To play something, dump it from media you own:

* **Game Boy / Color / Advance cartridges:** use a cartridge reader (open-hardware readers such as GBxCart RW, or commercial ones). Save files (`.sav`) come from the same readers.
* **PlayStation 1 discs:** rip with an external USB optical drive to `.cue/.bin` (one `.bin` per track), keeping audio tracks.
* **PlayStation BIOS:** dump it from your own console with homebrew tools. (An open-source BIOS replacement may be offered later as an optional download, with lower compatibility.)
* **Game Boy Advance BIOS:** optional; Attic has a built-in replacement for common functions.
* **Windows games:** install them through your own Steam, GOG or Epic accounts inside a Wine bottle.
